- **Google's TurboQuant Cuts LLM Memory Overhead at ICLR 2026.** Google's research team unveiled TurboQuant, an algorithm that significantly reduces the memory cost of the KV cache in large language models through a two-step process combining PolarQuant vector rotation and Quantized Johnson-Lindenstrauss compression. This matters because KV cache is one of the dominant memory bottlenecks during LLM inference at scale, and a practical compression method directly reduces serving costs and enables longer context at the same hardware budget.
