## ⚖️ Policy & Regulation

- **Illinois signs landmark AI Safety Measures Act.** Governor Pritzker signed the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act on July 6, modeling it on California and New York legislation. It requires AI developers to publish risk frameworks for "catastrophic risk," mandates incident reporting within 72 hours (or 24 hours for imminent risk), and requires independent third-party safety audits free of financial conflicts of interest. Illinois is now regarded as setting a national standard for enforceable AI duties.
