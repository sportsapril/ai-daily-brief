- **Goodfire interpretability audit suggests neurons are more monosemantic than assumed.** Goodfire published a preliminary audit of Qwen3-32B indicating that roughly 90% of neurons have a single primary function, which the authors argue weakens the dominant "polysemanticity" theory of neural network representations. *Why it matters:* If confirmed, this would upend a major assumption in mechanistic interpretability research — the idea that understanding neural networks is fundamentally hard because individual neurons encode many unrelated concepts.
