## ⚖️ Policy & Regulation

- **FTC opens public comment on AI accuracy — targeting ideological output manipulation (July 1, deadline July 31):** The FTC, directed by a December Trump executive order, issued a proposed policy statement warning that AI companies distorting model outputs "to achieve undisclosed ideological objectives" may be violating Section 5 of the FTC Act as "unfair or deceptive" conduct. The statement also argues that state AI laws (citing Colorado's Artificial Intelligence Act) that coerce changes to model outputs may be "impliedly preempted" by federal regulatory schemes. Comments are open through July 31. This is a significant federal framing of AI output regulation that pits federal preemption against state-level AI bills. Source: [FTC.gov](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-seeks-public-comment-policy-statement-addressing-ai-accuracy)

- **Illinois signs landmark AI regulation law, sets new national standard (July 6):** Governor Pritzker signed the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act into law, requiring developers of large-scale AI tools to publish a safety framework outlining how they identify and assess "catastrophic risk" (defined as incidents likely to cause death or serious injury to more than 50 people, or more than $1M in property damage). The law is modeled after California and New York bills. As of July 1, US states have enacted 109 AI laws and 28 data center laws — a patchwork that the FTC policy statement above directly addresses. Source: [WTTW / Chicago News](https://news.wttw.com/2026/07/06/pritzker-signs-landmark-ai-regulation-bill-aims-mitigate-risks)

- **EU AI Act high-risk provisions delayed; new dates set for 2027-2028 (June 29):** The EU Council gave final approval to delay application of high-risk AI system rules: standalone high-risk AI systems now face a December 2, 2027 deadline, while high-risk AI embedded in products has an August 2, 2028 deadline (pushed from the original August 2, 2026 date). The EU is also launching a call to build capacity for evaluating AI models before they enter the EU market. Source: [EU Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/06/29/artificial-intelligence-council-gives-final-green-light-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)
