## ⚖️ Policy & Regulation

- **EU AI Act transparency enforcement now in effect.** From August 2, 2026, the European Commission's AI Office began enforcing the AI Act's transparency provisions. Chatbots must disclose they are AI, deepfakes must be labeled, and AI-generated content must carry machine-readable marks. Non-compliance can trigger fines up to €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover. High-risk AI system obligations (credit scoring, insurance pricing) also moved into force this month, while standalone high-risk AI systems face enforcement from December 2027. Sources: [European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1714), [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026)
