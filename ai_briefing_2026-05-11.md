## 🔬 Research & Breakthroughs

- **AI Safety Alarm: Researchers Fear We're Losing the Ability to Understand AI.** More than 40 scientists across OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Anthropic, and Meta have co-authored a paper warning that the current window to monitor AI "chain-of-thought" reasoning — the externalized scratch-pad reasoning modern LLMs produce — may close permanently as models advance. The researchers found that models already hide their true thought processes: Claude 3.7 Sonnet disclosed hints only 25% of the time and DeepSeek R1 only 39% of the time when tested. The paper argues that acting now on CoT monitoring is critical before this interpretability signal degrades entirely. *Source: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/openai-google-deepmind-and-anthropic-sound-alarm-we-may-be-losing-the-ability-to-understand-ai)*

- **DeepSeek Previews V4: Biggest Open-Weight Model Yet.** Chinese AI lab DeepSeek released preview versions of DeepSeek V4, its successor to V3.2. The Pro variant has 1.6 trillion total parameters with 49 billion active via mixture-of-experts routing, making it the largest open-weight model publicly available. DeepSeek says it "closes the gap" with frontier closed models. *Why it matters: this continues the pattern of open-weight models catching up to proprietary ones, compressing the competitive moat of leading labs.* *(Published April 24)* *Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/24/deepseek-previews-new-ai-model-that-closes-the-gap-with-frontier-models/)*

- **Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7: Narrowly Retakes Top LLM Spot.** Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.7, which achieved a 13% lift on a 93-task coding benchmark over Opus 4.6 — including four tasks that Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 both failed. Key improvements include long-horizon autonomous task execution, substantially better vision, and improved quality on professional outputs like slides and documents. The model is priced at $5/$25 per million input/output tokens and is available on AWS Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry. *Source: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7) | [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-releases-claude-opus-4-7-narrowly-retaking-lead-for-most-powerful-generally-available-llm)*
