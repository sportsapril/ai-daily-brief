- **Project Glasswing: 10,000+ Vulnerabilities Found in 30 Days.** Anthropic's defensive cybersecurity initiative using the unreleased Claude Mythos Preview model identified over 10,000 high- or critical-severity vulnerabilities in critical software within its first 30 days.
