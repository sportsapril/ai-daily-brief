## ⚖️ Policy & Regulation

- **Illinois signs comprehensive AI Safety Measures Act.** Governor Pritzker signed Senate Bill 315 into law on July 6, setting some of the strictest state-level AI requirements in the U.S. The law requires large-scale AI developers to publish risk frameworks covering "catastrophic harm" (death/serious injury to 50+ people or $1M+ in property damage) and mandates independent third-party safety audits with no financial conflicts of interest — a first-of-its-kind requirement. Source: [WTTW Chicago](https://news.wttw.com/2026/07/06/pritzker-signs-landmark-ai-regulation-bill-aims-mitigate-risks), [mystateline.com](https://www.mystateline.com/news/local-news/illinois-sets-national-standard-for-ai-regulation-with-new-law/)

- **FTC opens public comment on AI accuracy policy.** The Federal Trade Commission issued a policy statement on the legal implications of state laws requiring AI systems to alter their "truthful outputs," with public comments due July 31, 2026. The move signals the FTC's intent to shape how truth and accuracy mandates interact with AI model behavior across jurisdictions. Source: [FTC.gov](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-seeks-public-comment-policy-statement-addressing-ai-accuracy)

- **EU delays high-risk AI rules; UN holds global governance dialogue.** The EU Council approved a delay in applying high-risk AI provisions: stand-alone high-risk systems now face a December 2027 compliance date, while AI embedded in products has until August 2028. Separately, the UN held a Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva on July 6–7, focused on international coordination on managing AI risk. Source: [EU Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/06/29/artificial-intelligence-council-gives-final-green-light-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/), [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/07/1167862)
